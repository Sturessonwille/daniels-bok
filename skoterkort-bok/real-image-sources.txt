Real free-to-use photos — Förarbevis snöskoter
==============================================

WHAT THE BOOK ACTUALLY USES (copyright-safe for a lecture book)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Third-party photos, even with CC BY / CC BY-SA, are still copyrighted.
This book therefore uses only:

  1. Customer-owned photos (Tänndalens skoteruthyrning, assets/Maskiner)
  2. Official Swedish road-sign artwork (public domain)
  3. Original diagrams drawn for this book
  4. Images generated for this book (not copied from a photographer)

That is why Commons / Unsplash / Pexels files listed below were NOT
dropped into assets/ for print.

Searched Wikimedia Commons, Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, Flickr CC,
Nordic / Swedish open archives, and US/UK government collections.

License notes (research only, not used in the printed book):
- CC BY / CC BY-SA: still copyrighted. Legal to reuse only with credit,
  and CC BY-SA requires share-alike on that photo if you adapt it.
- Public domain / CC0: no copyright left.
- Unsplash / Pexels / Pixabay: still copyrighted, with a stock licence.
- Best extra source you already have: the customer's own fleet
  (assets/Maskiner). Photograph those for engine, controls, types,
  riding poses and a stuck sled. Those shots beat stock.
