AI image prompts — Förarbevis snöskoter
=======================================

Use these to regenerate the AI photos in another program.
Do not generate text, captions, banners, arrows or labels in the image.
The book adds Swedish labels afterwards.

Style for all photos unless a prompt says otherwise:
- photorealistic, sharp, natural winter light
- Swedish mountain winter (Härjedalen / Funäsfjällen look)
- modern Ski-Doo-style touring snowmobile is fine (yellow or dark green)
- no logos you do not own, no readable fake brand names
- 16:9 landscape unless noted
- helmet on whenever someone is riding

=============================

FILE: assets/skotertyper.png
WHY THE CURRENT ONE FAILS
  Five sleds look almost the same. The lesson is track length and ski width.
  Mountain must look long-tracked and narrow. Work sled must look utility.

PROMPT
  Photorealistic studio lineup of five clearly different snowmobiles on a
  seamless beige background, three-quarter front-left view so tracks and ski
  stance are obvious. Left to right:
  1) Touring: two-up passenger seat with backrest, tall windshield, medium
     track, wide ski stance.
  2) Sport: low windshield, no passenger backrest, short track, sporty
     crouched stance.
  3) Mountain: no windshield, very long tall-lug track extending far behind
     the tunnel, narrow ski stance, running boards, mountain handlebar.
  4) Crossover: medium windshield, medium-long track, between sport and
     mountain.
  5) Utility / work: extra-wide track, cargo box on the tunnel, hitch,
     tall windshield, heavier stance.
  Differences in track length and ski width must be obvious at a glance.
  No text, no arrows, no watermark. 16:9.

WHY IT MUST SHOW THIS
  Rider weight INTO the slope (uphill / mountain side), not sitting
  neutrally.

PROMPT
  Photorealistic snowmobile traversing a steep snow slope from left to
  right. Rider in black helmet leans the body UPHILL into the mountain,
  not downhill. Machine is on a clear sidehill. Swedish fells. No text. 1:1.

PANEL 2 — icy downhill
WHY IT MUST SHOW THIS
  Going downhill on a shiny icy track, slight forward motion / throttle,
  not locked-up or airborne.

PROMPT
  Photorealistic snowmobile coming toward the camera down a narrow icy
  packed trail, slight spray from the track showing it is still turning.
  Rider in black helmet, weight slightly back, controlled speed. Swedish
  mountains. No text. 1:1.

PANEL 3 — jackknife / fällkniv
WHY THE CURRENT ONE IS WEAK
  The pulk has swung around, which is good, but the hitch looks broken
  and the pulk sits beside the skis. Need a clear fold: pulk coming
  alongside the snowmobile, hitch bent about 90 degrees.

PROMPT
  Photorealistic overhead three-quarter view of a snowmobile on a snow
  slope with a black plastic pulk jackknifing: the pulk has swung almost
  parallel to the snowmobile, hitch folded about 90 degrees, pulk coming
  up alongside the track. Rider looking back at the folding sled. This
  is the dangerous jackknife, not a pulk tracking straight. No text. 16:9.
